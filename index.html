FAQs

Before you come

What should I bring?

A swimsuit if you plan to use the steam room, and that's genuinely it. Robe, slippers, towels, lockers and toiletries are all here. Leave jewellery at home if you can.

What do I wear during treatments?

You undress to your comfort level and stay covered by towels throughout — only the area being worked on is uncovered. Plenty of people keep their underwear on for massages. Your therapist leaves the room while you get on the table.

Can I come if I'm pregnant?

Yes, after the first trimester. Book the prenatal massage rather than a standard one, and skip the steam room and the seaweed wrap. Let us know when you book so we set the room up properly.

Do you do gift vouchers?

Any amount, valid two years, and they can go towards packages or single treatments. We'll post them or email a printable one the same day.

What's the cancellation policy?

Forty-eight hours for spa days and twenty-four for single treatments, since a full day blocks a room and a therapist. Inside that we charge 50%.

Can I book for a group?

Up to six, but not on Saturdays — there isn't enough room in the lounge to keep it quiet for everyone else. Sunday and midweek are fine, and we can put aside a corner of the lounge.

Is there a minimum age?

Sixteen for treatments with a parent's consent, eighteen unaccompanied. The steam room is eighteen and over regardless.

Is the building accessible?

Ground floor has three treatment rooms, the lounge and an accessible bathroom. The steam room and two rooms are on the first floor and there is no lift — mention it when you book and we'll put you downstairs.
