The Serein Method

Map, course, hold

Three stages, and we tell you at the end of the first whether the second is worth your money.

Map

Twenty minutes, no charge

A full reading under magnification, photographs for the record, and a patch test if anything we'd use warrants one. You leave with a written plan whether or not you book.

Course

Usually six, a week apart

Spacing matters more than intensity. We adjust actives and pressure each session based on how you presented that day, not on what the plan said in week one.

Hold

Something you'll actually keep up

A maintenance plan built around what you'll realistically do at home, which is usually three products and not eleven. Reviewed every six months.

FAQs

Before you book

Why don't you list prices?

Because the honest answer to most enquiries is "it depends what your skin is doing". We quote at the end of the free consultation, in writing, and there's no pressure to book on the day.

Do I have to buy a course?

No. Single appointments are available for everything. We're upfront that courses are what actually move the needle, but plenty of people come in once a season and that's fine.

What happens at the consultation?

Twenty minutes. A reading under magnification, photographs for your record, a patch test if needed, and a written plan. It's free and you're not obliged to book anything.

Is any of this medical?

No. We're a beauty studio — no injectables, no needles, no prescriptions. Everything here is topical, manual or wax. If what you want needs a clinician we'll say so and point you elsewhere.

What's the cancellation policy?

Twenty-four hours and there's no charge. Inside that we charge 50%, because the room and the therapist are both blocked. Course sessions don't expire for twelve months.

Can I use my course with different therapists?

You can, but most people don't. Tell the desk if you'd rather stay with one person and we'll build the whole course around their diary.

Is the studio accessible?

We're on the first floor above a record shop and there's no lift, which we know is a problem. Email us and we will make arrangements — we have a ground-floor room available by appointment two doors down.
