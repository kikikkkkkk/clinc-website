Firming

We keep one serum in the building. There is no retail shelf, no second line to compare it against, and no margin in recommending it to you.

The serum is Bio Rajeu. Single-use vials, 1.5 mL, opened in front of you and mixed with the applicator at the start of each session. Nothing is decanted and nothing is kept between appointments.

It is a firming formulation rather than a resurfacing one, so it does not exfoliate and there is nothing to recover from. You can come on a lunch hour.

03

Studio

Opened 2018 with two rooms and one therapist. Three rooms and four of us now, which is as large as it is going to get — past that you stop knowing whose skin is whose, and the point of the place is that we remember.

Every consultation is run by Nadia Okonjo. Nobody is paid a percentage of what they sell, which is the only thing worth knowing about how the advice works here.

This is beauty work — topical, manual, wax. No injectables, no needles, no prescriptions. Where something needs a clinician we say so and point you at one.

Rooms are single-occupancy and quiet. We do not play music unless you ask for it, and we do not talk through a treatment unless you start.

Nadia told me at the consultation that two of the four things I came in asking for weren't worth doing.
