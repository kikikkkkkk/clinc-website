01 The studio

Serein opened in 2018 with two rooms and one therapist. There are three rooms and four of us now, which is as large as it is going to get — past that you stop knowing whose skin is whose, and the whole point of the place is that we remember.

Nadia Okonjo runs the studio and does every consultation herself. Between the four of us there are forty years in skin, body and brows. Everyone is licensed in Texas, and nobody is paid a percentage of what they sell you, which is the single most useful thing we can tell you about how the advice works.

What we do is beauty work: topical, manual, and wax. No injectables, no needles, no prescriptions. If what you came in for needs a clinician, we will say so and point you at one rather than sell you the nearest thing we happen to stock.

The room is on the first floor and there is no lift, which we know is a problem. Email ahead and we will move you to the ground-floor room two doors down.

Nadia told me at the consultation that two of the four things I came in asking for weren't worth doing.

03 Signature course

The firming course

Sculpted. Revived. Radiant.

Bio Rajeu is the one line we standardised the studio on, after eight months of trialling things that did not do what the literature said. 04 Visiting

The consultation
Twenty minutes and free. A reading under magnification, photographs for your record, a patch test where one is needed, and a written plan at the end. You are not obliged to book anything, and roughly a third of people do not.
Why there are no prices for some things
Because the honest answer to most enquiries is that it depends what your skin is doing. Where a price is fixed it is printed above. Where it is not, we quote in writing at the end of the consultation and you take it away to think about.
Getting here
1120 East 6th Street, above the record shop; the studio door is to the left of theirs. Street parking on East 6th, and the lot on Medina is free after six. First floor, no lift — email ahead and we will move you downstairs.
Cancelling
Twenty-four hours and there is no charge. Inside that we charge half, because the room and the therapist are both blocked out. Course sessions do not expire for twelve months, so a missed week is not a problem.
Staying with one therapist
You can move between us or stay with one. Tell the desk and we will build the whole course around that person's diary.
Gift vouchers
Any amount, valid two years, usable against courses or single appointments. Emailed the same day or posted if you would rather have the card.

Sixth course here. The photographs are the thing — you forget what your skin looked like in January.
